The mark is a shader graph: inputs in, a node, an output.

The symbol encodes the tool's atomic unit — the same port language used everywhere in the app. Hollow rings are input sockets, the solid accent disc is the node carrying a shader waveform, the small disc is its output plug. A single soft halo reads as emitted light. Precise and systemic, with just a spoon of play.
